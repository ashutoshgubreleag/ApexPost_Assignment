Feature: Shipment status lifecycle
  As a dispatch team
  I want each shipment to move through the delivery lifecycle in a valid order
  So that customers and couriers see the real progress of every parcel

  Background:
	Given a shipment "APX-10000001" exists for "Jane Citizen" in "Melbourne"

  Scenario Outline: A shipment advances through the allowed forward path
	Given the shipment has already been moved to "<fromStatus>"
	When the shipment is moved to "<nextStatus>"
	Then the shipment status is "<nextStatus>"

	Examples:
	  | fromStatus      | nextStatus     |
	  | Created         | PickedUp       |
	  | PickedUp       | InTransit      |
	  | InTransit       | OutForDelivery |
	  | OutForDelivery  | Delivered      |
	  | OutForDelivery  | Failed         |

  Scenario: A delivered shipment cannot be moved back to in transit
	Given the shipment has already been moved to "Delivered"
	When the shipment is moved to "InTransit"
	Then the status change is refused because the shipment cannot move backwards
	And the shipment status is "Delivered"

  Scenario: Skipping a lifecycle step is refused
	When the shipment is moved to "OutForDelivery"
	Then the status change is refused because the shipment must progress one step at a time
	And the shipment status is "Created"
